# revomkg - BMKG (Reimagined)

### **DISCLAIMER: This is an UNOFFICIAL movement.**

**revomkg** is not officially affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to **BMKG (Badan Meteorologi, Klimatologi, dan Geofisika)**. This is a ground-up, unofficial initiative started by a younger generation within BMKG who are passionate about positive change and innovation.

## Our Mission

We are a collective of individuals who see immense potential in modernizing and enhancing the tools and services that BMKG provides. Our goal is to "revolutionize" (in a good way!) our operational systems to better serve the Indonesian people, making our society safer and more prosperous.

This movement is about building, innovating, and improving. We aim to create updates or alternatives to operational software and applications that need enhancement.

Our areas of interest include (but are not limited to):

* **High-Performance Computing:** Migrating critical serial computations to parallel computations (e.g., from CPU to GPU) for faster results.

* **Modern UI/UX:** Re-designing interfaces to be more intuitive, accessible, and user-friendly for both internal operators and the public.

* **Security:** Hardening systems and implementing modern security best practices to protect critical data and services.

* **Anything Else!** If there's a process, app, or system that can be improved, we want to explore it.

## We Are Open Source

This is a community-driven effort. We believe in the power of collaboration and transparency. All projects under the `revomkg` organization are open source.

**Anyone can contribute.** Whether you are part of BMKG or just a passionate developer, designer, or data scientist who wants to help, your contributions are welcome.

## How to Contribute to Existing Projects

1. **Explore Projects:** Check out the repositories within this organization.

2. **Find an Issue:** Look for existing issues or "help wanted" tags.

3. **Fork & PR:** Fork the repository, make your changes in a new branch, and submit a **Pull Request**.

4. **Discuss:** Open an issue to discuss bugs, new features, or improvements.

## Got a New Project Idea?

Do you have a big idea for a **new project** or a brilliant suggestion for a major improvement that isn't in an existing repository? We want to hear it!
